Master the Adventure (EN)

Elevate your Dragonbane experience with the ultimate digital companion for Android. Focus on the story and the rolls, while we handle the tracking.

  • Hero Creation: Build your character from scratch in minutes.
  • Complete Tracking: Real-time management of HP, Willpower (WP), and Conditions.
  • Smart Dice Logic: Instant calculation of Banes and Boons for every skill and weapon.
  • Inventory & Load: Automated encumbrance calculation so you're never slowed down unexpectedly.
  • Spellbook: A complete grimoire to manage your magical powers and casting costs.

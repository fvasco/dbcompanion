Privacy Policy for DB Companion

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect any personal data directly.

The DB Companion application is designed to be a functional tool. While we do not manually collect or store your personal identity (like name or email), the app uses third-party automated tools for display features, performance analysis, and advertising that may process device identifiers or technical data.

All user-generated data (such as tables and settings) remains locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

This application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

1. Google Fonts

To ensure uniform and visually appealing rendering of fonts across different devices, this app uses external web fonts provided by Google Fonts. When you load a page or view content within the app, your browser or device connects directly to Google's servers to download the necessary fonts. During this connection, Google receives technical information, including your IP address, to serve the requested font files. For more information, please visit Google Fonts Privacy FAQ and the general Google Privacy Policy.

2. Google AdMob (Advertising)

To keep this application free, we use Google AdMob for displaying advertisements.

Transparency on Data Storage: In compliance with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF), this app treats the "TCF Purpose 1" (Storage and access of information on device) as the primary indicator of consent for all data storage, including Analytics Storage.

By providing consent through the app's consent banner, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • AdMob (Google) may collect and use information about your device and interests to provide personalized advertisements.

For more details on how Google processes advertising data, please refer to the Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed at anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. As stated above, the application does not directly collect any personal data from any user.
